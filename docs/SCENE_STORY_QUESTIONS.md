# Scene stories: 30 new questions

*Created 13 November 2026. These are **not** the earlier thirty technical/history questions in [QUESTIONS.md](QUESTIONS.md). They focus on visual storytelling, character identity, world-building, artistic decisions and what the creators have (and have not) revealed. All 30 have researched answers in the linked documents.*

**Evidence key:** **Canon** = explicitly stated by the maker; **Observed** = visible scene/independent contemporary review; **Interpretation** = reasoned reading, not a maker's explanation; **Unknown** = no reliable source established.

## FurMark — the furry doughnut ([answers](SCENE_FURMARK.md))

1. Why was **fur** chosen as the GPU-stressing material rather than a conventional game, explosion or fire?
2. Why is the object a **torus/doughnut** rather than a furry animal—and did furry doughnuts predate FurMark?
3. What did the first furry doughnut look like, and how did the visual idea develop?
4. Are the hairs real geometric strands, a shader illusion, or both?
5. Why does the doughnut rotate endlessly instead of appearing in a recognisable fictional world?
6. Why the orange/fire-like colour, checkerboard/dragon imagery and changing backgrounds?
7. How did an absurd-looking object become the dreaded 'power virus'?
8. What are the interlaced double torus and FurMark 2's knot meant to be?
9. What do the 'Hot Like Hell' heat haze and variable fur height tell us about FurMark's art?
10. Is there a secret story or character behind the doughnut—and why has it endured as an icon?

## UNIGINE Superposition — the vanished professor ([answers](SCENE_SUPERPOSITION.md))

1. **Who is the professor?** Is there an official name, biography or identity?
2. Why does he work in an **abandoned classroom** rather than a modern research laboratory?
3. What exactly happened in the laboratory just before the player arrived?
4. Why does the official story specifically mention **quantum theory**?
5. What are all the strange coils, instruments, mechanical rings, notes and portraits for?
6. Which visual details are confirmed **clues**, and which might simply be atmospheric props?
7. Can you actually play detective—pick up things, inspect notebooks and discover a story?
8. How do gravity, adjustable time, lighting and physics connect to the mystery?
9. Is there an official solution, ending or reveal explaining where the professor went?
10. Is the aesthetic based on Tesla, Einstein, steampunk, or a documented real-world scientist?

## 3DMark Steel Nomad — the wanderer ([answers](SCENE_STEEL_NOMAD.md))

1. **Who or what is the Steel Nomad?** Does the protagonist have a name or biography?
2. Where does the wanderer arrive from, and what is the **sequence of the journey**?
3. What sort of world is this—post-apocalypse, frontier, alien colony or something else?
4. Is the masked traveller a robot, a cyborg or a human wearing armour?
5. Who else lives in the settlement, and what do its caravans, machines and vehicles imply?
6. What is the glowing orb or blue projection, and what is the wanderer trying to do?
7. Why does **Steel Nomad Light** turn the desert into a nocturnal lake—does time pass?
8. What **hidden references** connect the scene to 25 years of earlier 3DMark worlds?
9. Why was sound brought back, and what can Explorer mode reveal beyond the scripted run?
10. Do UL's artists give the Nomad a complete story or deliberately leave the world mysterious?

## What counts as an answer?

A sourced *absence* of a published answer is worth recording: inventing the professor's name, deciding why the doughnut was chosen without a developer quote, or turning visual guesses into Steel Nomad canon would misrepresent these works. The answer files specify precisely which parts are known, inferred or unknown.
