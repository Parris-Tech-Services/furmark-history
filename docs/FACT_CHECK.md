## Verified as stated

FurMark: first release 2007 (developer's statement, repeated by KitGuru); 1.8.0 on 2 Feb 2010 with heat effect, displacement mapping, XML localization and lower scores; 1.8.2 on 19 Apr 2010; Catalyst 8.8 file-name check and 9.8 texture-to-ALU check; HD 5870 hardware chip throttling one PowerPlay level; GTX 580 on 9 Nov 2010 with 153 W limited and 304 W unlimited; the Jan 2011 poll post and PowerTune's roughly 20% range; the "right to draw some furry objects" quote; Kombustor wording; changelog dates for 1.17.0, 1.18.0, 2.0.0.0, 2.0.4.0 (Linux), 2.1.0.2, 2.3.0.0, 2.5.0.0 and 2.11.0.4; KitGuru's 26 Feb 2024 story.

3DMark: Final Reality 1997, company founded Nov 1997 and launched 27 Feb 1998, rename to Futuremark in 2002, the Rightware, Rovio and UL dates, version-table dates, Port Royal, Speed Way, Steel Nomad, macOS, Solar Bay Extreme, and the June and July 2026 posts.

Superposition: UNIGINE's 2005 founding and benchmark list, Heaven's 22 Oct 2009 announcement, the 1.1 version history, presets, editions and scoring from the manual.
