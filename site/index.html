FurMark
by Jérôme “JeGX” Guinot
  • 2007 first released as a fur-rendering OpenGL benchmark
  • Treated as a “power virus”: AMD and Nvidia built protections around it
  • FurMark 2 on GeeXLab: OpenGL, Vulkan, Linux, Raspberry Pi
  • Newest build: 2.11.0.4 (30 Oct 2026)

  • 2005 company founded; roots in the frustum.org project of 2002
  • Sanctuary → Tropics → Heaven → Valley → Superposition

3DMark
from Final Reality to path tracing
  • 1998 3DMark99, “the world’s first benchmark for gamers”
  • One new test per DirectX: Time Spy, Port Royal, Speed Way
  • Steel Nomad (2024): native 4K, score = average FPS × 100
  • A fully path-traced successor announced in June 2026

FurMark points ↔ FPS

The developer’s paired numbers (6478 points at 108 FPS) show points are frames rendered in the 60-second benchmark. That is an inference, not a stated formula.

—

Steel Nomad / Speed Way score ↔ FPS

UL: score = average graphics-test FPS × 100. Steel Nomad renders at 3840 × 2160; Speed Way at 2560 × 1440.

—

Time Spy overall score

A weighted harmonic mean: graphics weight 0.85, CPU weight 0.15. A weak CPU drags the total down more than an average would.
