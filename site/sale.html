eBay selling plan · 16 November 2026

Pilot: prepare physical GPU #18 first, then #19 and #20, then #24. The resale grades and individual copyable listing drafts are generated from your separate read-only benchmark extract. A benchmark grade is not a warranty or a full hardware health test.

  • Trial price: A$139 Buy It Now with Best Offer (plus postage). Consider offers around A$110–125 only after checking current demand and your total costs. This is a starting experiment, not a guaranteed sale price. Australian GTX 1070 listings have shown a wide spread: a September 2026 Gigabyte auction ended at A$70.64 including buyer protection, while higher-priced models are advertised around A$135–180.
  • Before posting: attach the numbered GPU label, photograph front/back/model sticker and outputs, confirm actual board maker in person, save a dated OCCT memory-test report and check every display output and fan under load. Do not publish serial numbers or claim a memory-test pass from a generic “No errors” note.
  • Packaging: put the bare card in an antistatic shielding bag, immobilise it in a padded rigid cardboard box, and protect connectors and fan shroud. Do not ship a GPU loose in a soft satchel.
  • Postage: Australia Post's July 2026 domestic retail rates list A$16 postage for other packaging over 500 g to 1 kg and A$20.25 for over 1 kg to 3 kg, before purchasing the box and protective materials. Measure the fully packed parcel, check cubic weight, and confirm the exact eBay label quote before listing.
  • eBay rules: don't create multiple simultaneous identical fixed-price listings. eBay restricts multi-quantity and variations to Pro sellers. Eligible Australia-based non-Pro sellers under the A$25,000 rolling annual threshold may qualify for fee-free sales, but check your own seller status and label requirements.
  • Holds: #36 noisy fan; #40 previous test-PC NVIDIA BSOD (GPU cause unconfirmed); #4 has a mismatched public result link. The latest unverified result links must be audited before a sale-ready grade is given.

Sources: September 2026 sold auction · GTX 1070 asking prices · Australia Post July 2026 prices · eBay duplicate listings · eBay selling fees.
