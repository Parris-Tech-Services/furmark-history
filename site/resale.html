    Limits

    Benchmarks show a card initialised and finished three short high-load runs. They do not prove memory correctness, display outputs, fan condition or reliability over time. The public results report PCI subsystem 7377:0000 (Colorful), which conflicts with the Gigabyte label, so confirm the board maker from each card before a brand-specific listing. Prices are asking ranges, not sold prices. Full method and limits.
