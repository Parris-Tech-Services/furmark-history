These were gaps in the original histories when the question list was created. All 30 now have evidence-based answers in FurMark, Superposition and 3DMark. Answers distinguish sourced facts, reviewer opinions, inference and questions that remain genuinely unverified. A researched question does not imply every historical unknown has been resolved.

FurMark

  1. How is the fur actually rendered, and what do JeGX's own tutorials say about the technique?
  2. What are FurMark's presets and burn-in modes, and how is its score calculated?
  3. What is GeeXLab, and how did it shape FurMark 2?
  4. Has FurMark ever verifiably damaged a graphics card, or is that myth?
  5. Do modern GPUs and drivers (RTX, RDNA) still detect or limit FurMark, and how does power limiting work today?
  6. What did FurMark 1.9.0's "even heavier load" and the Xtreme burning mode change?
  7. Who is JeGX, and what else did Geeks3D and oZone3D build (GPU Caps Viewer, GPU Shark, GPU-Z links)?
  8. How does FurMark behave on Raspberry Pi and Arm, and why was that added?
  9. How does FurMark compare with other stress tools (OCCT, Kombustor, Heaven, Time Spy Stress Test) on heat and power?
  10. What do online FurMark scores and the comparative score table show, and how big is the database?

Superposition

  1. Who is UNIGINE (where based, how big), and what is the UNIGINE 2 engine?
  2. What do typical Superposition scores look like across GPU generations, and who holds records?
  3. How does the stress test and VR-ready test work, and how do reviewers use them?
  4. Why does Superposition use DirectX 11 and OpenGL rather than Vulkan or DX12, and did UNIGINE add newer APIs to the engine?
  5. What are the histories of Heaven and Valley, and are they still used?
  6. What is the claim about an 8K Superposition demo broadcast from space?
  7. Which review sites and test suites (Phoronix, Tom's Hardware, others) adopted Superposition?
  8. How does SSRTGI work according to UNIGINE's own documentation?
  9. What do the Advanced and Pro editions cost, and how is it licensed?
  10. How does Superposition rank against competing GPU benchmarks in overclocking competitions?

3DMark

  1. How are Time Spy and Steel Nomad scores calculated, and what do the tests actually render?
  2. How big is 3DMark's user base and result database, and who holds records?
  3. What were the 3DMark03 Nvidia driver cheating scandal and the smartphone delistings?
  4. What was the Time Spy asynchronous compute controversy?
  5. Which 3DMark feature tests exist (DXR, mesh shaders, DLSS, VRS, sampler feedback) and when did they arrive?
  6. What are the mobile tests (Ice Storm, Sling Shot, Wild Life, Solar Bay) and their history?
  7. What did 3DMark cost over the years, and how has the Steam business gone?
  8. What other benchmarks did Futuremark make (PCMark, VRMark, Peacekeeper), and what was the 3DMark Vantage PhysX dispute?
  9. How does the Benchmark Development Program work, and who are its members?
  10. How were Port Royal and Speed Way received, and how do their scores relate to real games?
